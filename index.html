MAIZE CONGREGATIONAL CHURCH







We are an independent congregation of believers seeking to model the

First Century Church in our devotion to the teaching of the apostles, fellowship,

the Lord's Supper and prayer. (Acts 2:42)

Matthew 28:18-20


And Jesus came and said to them, “All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age.”

WE BELIEVE

Salvation is the free gift of God received through faith in Jesus Christ alone. All scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness. Every believer enjoys absolute certainty of an eternal home in heaven with Jesus and the Father.

MISSIONS

  • • Locally we support Embrace, and Union Rescue Mission. Internationally we support Voice of the Martyrs and Trans World Radio (TWR).
  • • Each year, we build a home for a family in need in Juarez Mexico with Casas por Cristo.
  • • We host Vacation Bible School for children along with Teen Camp each summer.
